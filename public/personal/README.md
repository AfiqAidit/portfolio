# Personal photos for "Know me better"

Drop JPG or WebP files here and list them under `photos` on the matching fact in `src/content/personal.ts`, for example:

```ts
{
  icon: "feather",
  label: "Sports",
  value: "Badminton",
  photos: [{ src: "badminton.jpg", alt: "Afiq playing badminton at a local court" }],
},
```

- Up to 3 photos per fact; they show side by side in a 16:10 frame, cropped to fill
- Landscape photos work best; keep each file under about 500 KB
- For games, use your own in-game screenshots rather than official artwork
- Avoid faces of friends who haven't agreed to be on the site
